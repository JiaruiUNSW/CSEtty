## COMP1521

- Course and resource index: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/>
- Course outline: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/outline/>
- Public exam/revision overview: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/slides/week_10/COMP1521_Wk_10_Lec_2_Exam_and_Revision.pdf>
- Released practice-exam index: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/resources/practice_exams.html>
- MIPS guide: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/resources/mips-guide.html>
- Linux cheat sheet: <https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs1521/26T1/resources/linux-cheatsheet.html>

The bank covers C revision, MIPS data/control/functions, integer representation,
bitwise operations, a small amount of floating point, POSIX files and metadata,
Unicode/UTF-8, directories and recursive traversal, processes, pipes, threads,
and synchronisation. The fixed 10-position generator blueprint is documented in
`QUESTION_BANK.md`.
